PROGRAMS OFFERED, CODES & ACCREDITATION:

1. B.Sc. Software Engineering (Program Code: SE)
- Accreditation: Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) accredited.
- Seats: 40–42 seats as per PEC-approved intake and University rules.
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * Minimum 60% marks in F.Sc. / HSSC (Pre-Engineering / ICS with Mathematics, Physics, Computer / DAE Computer or equivalent qualification with Mathematics certified by IBCC).
  * No 3rd division in entire academic career.
- Pre-Medical Policy for SE:
  * Maximum 40% of the intake may be filled by F.Sc. Pre-Medical candidates if they fulfill all merit criteria and PEC policy requirements.
  * Under general eligibility, Pre-Medical students are eligible for SET or CET without restriction, and can compete for SE under the PEC 40% quota if they have >= 60% marks.
- Mandatory Requirements: University Entrance Test (Entry Test) + Departmental Interview.
- Semester Tuition Fee: ~PKR 60,130 per semester.

2. B.Sc. Software Engineering Technology (Program Code: SET)
- Accreditation: National Technology Council (NTC) accredited.
- Seats: 45 seats as per NTC-approved intake and University rules.
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * Minimum 50% marks in F.Sc. / HSSC (Pre-Engineering / Pre-Medical / ICS with Mathematics, Physics, Computer / DAE Computer or equivalent qualification certified by IBCC).
- Pre-Medical Policy for SET:
  * F.Sc. Pre-Medical students with at least 50% marks are eligible under NTC policy, subject to passing the admission test and taking an additional non-credit / deficiency Mathematics course of F.Sc. level in the 1st semester.
- Mandatory Requirements: University Entrance Test (Entry Test) + Departmental Interview.
- Semester Tuition Fee: ~PKR 60,130 per semester.

3. B.Sc. Computer Engineering Technology (Program Code: CET)
- Accreditation: National Technology Council (NTC) accredited.
- Eligibility Criteria:
  * Minimum 50% marks in F.Sc. / HSSC (Pre-Engineering / Pre-Medical / ICS with Mathematics, Physics, Computer / DAE Computer / DAE Electrical / DAE Electronics or equivalent qualification certified by IBCC).
- Pre-Medical Policy for CET:
  * Same as SET: F.Sc. Pre-Medical students with at least 50% marks are eligible under NTC policy, subject to passing the admission test and taking an additional non-credit / deficiency Mathematics course in the 1st semester.
- Mandatory Requirements: University Entrance Test (Entry Test) + Departmental Interview.
- Semester Tuition Fee: ~PKR 59,130 per semester.

4. MS Software Engineering (Postgraduate)
- Duration: 2-year program (4 semesters).
- Eligibility: 16 years of education in relevant field with minimum 50% marks or 2.0 CGPA + GAT/UGAT (50%).
- Semester Tuition Fee: ~PKR 57,590 per semester.

- Original Documents Policy: Original documents must be brought for physical verification during the interview. Original CNIC/Form-B + admit card are strictly required at the Entry Test examination hall. Public sector employees require a study leave certificate or departmental NOC.

ADMISSION PROCESS & STEPS:
1. Online Registration: Apply online via the official portal https://uajk.edu.pk/ or submit hardcopy at DSA office.
2. Fee Challan: Deposit application and test fee at any Habib Bank Limited (HBL) branch.
3. Entrance Test: Download admit card and appear for the university entrance test at Chehla Campus.
4. Departmental Interview: Bring original documents for verification.
5. Merit List: Provisional and final merit lists published on the university website and department notice board.
6. Fee Submission & Enrollment: Pay semester admission fee upon selection to secure admission.
